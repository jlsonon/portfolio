## Product Principles
1. **Real Software for Real Operations**: Focus on business impact, uptime, and operational throughput over superficial visual gimmicks.
2. **Native Ergonomics & Zero Friction**: Native OS cursor mechanics, sub-second initial load, and accessible keyboard navigation.
3. **Provable Credibility**: Every case study and claim must be backed by live applications, concrete architecture, and verified metrics.
4. **Distinct Architectural Aesthetic**: Warm, high-contrast, confident visual rhythm without generic AI template defaults.
